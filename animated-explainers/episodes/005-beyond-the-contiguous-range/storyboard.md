# Beyond the contiguous range

*Multi-segment and cross-block annotations*  
Concepts · Volume I — The Nature of Text · 005

This film is for readers learning to model language and literary texts. Follow the word “up” in the first example and the closing quotation mark in the second. The first moves past words outside the annotation. The second moves with its block while every quoted word remains inside the annotation.

## Worked text

> She picked the book up.

Annotation A owns “picked” and “up”. The opening uses the ordinary word order “She picked up the book.” so the viewer can follow the same lexical unit from a continuous to a discontinuous target.

> She began to read aloud: “There was once a garden. And in that garden grew a remarkable tree.”

Annotation Q begins and ends on the quotation marks. Divide the text after “garden.” The space between sentences becomes a structural division and returns as the original space Cell when the blocks rejoin. The two lower display lines wrap within the same segment.
