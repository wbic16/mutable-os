# Where text meets the graph

*Standoff annotation as a bridge between text and knowledge*  
Mutable OS / Concepts / 006 · Volume II — From Text to Knowledge

The film is for readers learning how a document can participate in a knowledge system. Follow “The Emperor” in Documents 02 and 05. Each mention record can be followed to a specific person and back to the passage where the words occur.
