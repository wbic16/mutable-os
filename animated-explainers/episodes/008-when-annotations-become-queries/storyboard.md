## Worked text and readings

In “Napoleon reportedly entered Paris.” the two mentions cover “Napoleon” and “Paris”. Reported information covers “reportedly entered Paris”. The arrival event covers “Napoleon reportedly entered Paris”. The final full stop lies outside all four ranges.

The research corpus contains five original illustrative passages. Speech includes its quotation marks. The supplied entity references and speech boundaries determine each result.

The final ontology study uses a separate illustrative sentence, “A fine victory for Napoleon,” she said. An editor has marked “fine victory” as irony. The speech contains both that expression and the person mention. Each has its own relation to the speech.
