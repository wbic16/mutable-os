# Beyond the Hierarchy

*Overlapping structures and coexisting ontologies*  
Mutable Concepts · Volume I — Foundations · 003 · 75 seconds · narrated and silent editions

> The moon rose slowly  
> above the sleeping sea.

For a reader meeting standoff annotation for the first time, the observable result is being able to follow four boundaries in order. Start the first line at **The**, enter the predicate at **rose**, leave the line at **slowly**, then leave the predicate at the full stop. No enclosure can nest this pair intact. Two tracks can carry both spans with their original endpoints.

The second half asks what each track means. One system describes poetic lines, another grammatical roles, another rhetorical figures. A literary system can join them. Two readers using that system can still give different accounts of the same image.

The line break remains in the passage. The straightened view reserves one space for it. The first line ends on the **y** of *slowly*. The predicate includes the final full stop by the convention supplied for this example. Both reader annotations end before that full stop.

The stanza contains both lines even after the independent analyses arrive. The example concerns the containment rule of one element tree. Vocabularies can share a text while retaining their own definitions. Personification is a rhetorical reading of the sea as a sleeper. Tranquillity and latent power are attributed possibilities for the atmosphere this image creates.
