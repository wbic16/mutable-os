## Range decisions

Mint identifies both character names. Amber covers “met the White Rabbit”. Violet covers the opening action “Alice met”. The violet span starts first and ends inside the amber span. The period belongs solely to the original text.

The example omits the suggested whole-sentence Encounter annotation so the crossing pair can be read at phone size. The sentence settles into position during the last 1.2 seconds of character labelling and stays fixed throughout separation, multiplicity and recombination. The brief’s seven cues govern timing.
